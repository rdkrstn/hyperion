01Lead & Inquiry Capture

Every inquiry enters one lane

This is the intake layer. Facebook comments, Messenger inquiries, landing page forms, QR kiosks, and phone calls are standardized into one record before sales touches the lead.

02MSME Energy Audit & Sizing Engine

Engineer time stays protected

The pre-audit turns a monthly bill into a first-pass system size, savings estimate, payback range, and survey summary. It is not a final engineering design. It decides whether the lead deserves a site visit.

Drop-off diagnosis
  • High comment volume does not matter unless every inquiry becomes a trackable record.
  • Site survey no-shows fall when bills, location, and roof access are requested before the visit.
  • Proposal follow-up needs a stage, an owner, and a deadline. Memory is not a sales system.
  • Financing friction should be visible by score band and business type.
