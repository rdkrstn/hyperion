# Code of Conduct

Hyperion uses a simple contributor standard: be respectful, constructive, and specific.

## Expected Behavior

- Keep discussions focused on the work.
- Give actionable feedback.
- Assume good intent while still being precise about risks.
- Respect privacy and never post real customer data or credentials.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, threats, or personal attacks.
- Publishing private credentials, customer data, or exploit details.
- Repeatedly derailing technical discussions.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove comments, close issues, or restrict participation when behavior harms the project or its contributors.
